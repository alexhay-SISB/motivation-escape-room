Built for pairs. Powered by your knowledge.

Nicknames. No student accounts.

Use a made-up nickname, not a full name. Only your nickname, puzzle answers and game progress are sent to your teacher’s browser. Your partner sees your nickname and progress.

No email, Google sign-in, camera, microphone, analytics or advertising. The game does not save a student database. A random rejoin token and nickname stay in this tab’s session storage until you leave or close it.

The free PeerJS signalling service and STUN services help browsers connect. Like other web infrastructure, these services and GitHub receive normal connection information such as IP addresses. This is not a promise of zero technical data processing.

The teacher’s tab must stay open. School networks that block WebRTC may prevent live play; use the shared-device practice mode in that case. Ending a class clears its in-memory roster and progress.
